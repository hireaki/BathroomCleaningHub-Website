HOW TO CLEAN A MIRROR

PRODUCTS & ITEMS USED

 Air Freshener

All-Purpose Cleaner

Step 1: Spray the mirror


Begin by selecting a glass cleaner, such as Windex. Hold the spray bottle and spray an even layer of cleaner across the entire surface, ensuring all areas are covered, especially spots with visible stains or marks.


Step 2: Wipe the mirror


Using a clean, soft cloth, wipe the mirror in gentle circular motions. This technique helps lift dirt and residue without leaving streaks.


Step 3: Give the mirror extra shine


After wiping away the cleaner, switch to the dry part of the cloth or use a separate clean, dry cloth to make the mirror shiny and clear.


Step 4: Dry the mirror


Once the mirror is thoroughly cleaned and buffed, give it a moment to air dry completely, and you’re done!
