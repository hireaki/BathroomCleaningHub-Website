BATHROOM CLEANING LIFE HACKS


Prevent Foggy Mirrors


Buff a small shaving foam onto your mirror, then wipe it clean. This will prevent it from fogging up the mirror.


Remove limestone deposits


Mix dish soap and vinegar in equal parts (50/50). Use this solution to cut through tough limestone deposits on sinks, tiles, and other surfaces. It easily breaks down the grime and deposits.


Remove limestone deposits


Use a flat-head mop to clean wall tiles. It helps to reach high or hard-to-clean areas with ease.


Whiten Sinks and Toilets


Drop a denture tablet into the sink or toilet and let it soak. It helps remove stains and whiten surfaces without the need for scrubbing.
