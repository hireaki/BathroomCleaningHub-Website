HOW TO CLEAN TILES

Step 1: Apply and Wait Start by applying a tile cleaner evenly across the tiled surface, ensuring you cover all areas, including grout lines where dirt and stains often accumulate. Allow the cleaner to sit for a few minutes as directed on the label.



Step 2: Agitate with a brush After the cleaner has had time to work, take a stiff-bristled brush or a grout brush and scrub the tiles and grout lines. Use firm, circular motions to dislodge dirt and lift stains from the surface.



Step 3: Rinse with warm water Once you've scrubbed the tiles, rinse away the cleaning solution and loosened dirt using warm water. You can wipe down the tiles with a dry microfiber cloth after rinsing, which will help prevent water spots and leave the surface looking clean
