HOW TO CLEAN A SHOWERHEAD

PRODUCTS & ITEMS USED

 White Vinegar

Medium-sized Bowl

Step 1: Disconnect the showerhead from the shower


Carefully remove the showerhead from the shower arm by unscrewing it. Use an adjustable wrench or pliers if it's tightly secured.


Step 2: Fill a container white vinegar


Take a container large enough to hold the showerhead and fill it with white vinegar. Make sure the container is sturdy and placed on a stable surface to prevent spills.


Step 3: Submerge shower head


Place the shower head into the container, ensuring it is fully submerged in the vinegar. Allow it to soak for at least 12 hours. The vinegar will dissolve limescale, soap scum, and other buildups.


Step 4: Rinse under cool water


After soaking, remove the showerhead from the vinegar and rinse it thoroughly under cool running water, then reattach it to the shower arm.
