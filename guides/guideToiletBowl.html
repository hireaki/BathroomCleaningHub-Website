HOW TO CLEAN A TOILET BOWL

PRODUCTS & ITEMS USED

 Air Freshener

Step 1: Remove the toilet seat


Start by carefully detaching the toilet seat for a more thorough cleaning. Most seats can be removed by unscrewing the bolts at the base, either by hand or with a screwdriver.


Step 2: Clean the toilet seat


Spray the toilet seat thoroughly with a cleaning solution or disinfectant spray, covering all surfaces, including the top to bottom. Then, rinse the toilet seat with clean water to remove the cleaner.


Step 3: Apply the toilet cleaner


Pourthe toilet cleaner around the inside of the toilet bowl, focusing on areas under the rim and where stains or buildup are visible.


Step 4: Scrub the toilet


After scrubbing the bowl thoroughly, it's time to flush away all the dirt and cleaning products.


Step 5: Flush the Toilet


After scrubbing the bowl thoroughly, it's time to flush away all the dirt and cleaning products.


Step 6: Spray the entire area to disinfect


Spray a disinfectant like Lysol and other disinfectant on the toilet's exterior, including the tank, lid, base, and flush handle.


Step 7: Clean from top to bottom


Finish by wiping down the toilet from top to bottom, starting with the tank and lid, then moving to the seat, bowl, and base. Cleaning in this order ensures that dirt and germs do not spread to already-cleaned areas.
