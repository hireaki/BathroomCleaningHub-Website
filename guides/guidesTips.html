TIPS FOR CLEANING YOUR BATHROOM


Use the Right Tools and Products


Using the right tools and cleaners makes the job faster and easier. For example, glass cleaner can be used for mirrors and tile cleaner can be used for walls. The right supplies help remove dirt and stains more effectively and make the job easier.


Declutter


Move everything off the counters, shelves, and floor before you start cleaning. This makes it easier to clean every spot and keeps things neat.


Sanitize Areas that Often Touch


Clean things you touch a lot, like faucets, light switches, handles, and doorknobs. Use a disinfectant to kill germs and keep your bathroom safe and healthy.
